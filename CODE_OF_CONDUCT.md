# Code of Conduct

Redhot follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and patient, assume good intent, and keep criticism about the work, not the person. Harassment, insults, and personal or political attacks aren't accepted in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior concerns you, contact the maintainers privately through the repository's **Security** tab (**Report a vulnerability** works for conduct reports too), or use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment. Reports are kept confidential.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block anyone who breaks this code, following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
